Dwayne Douglas Johnson

born May 2, 1972

Dwayne "The Rock" Johnson is a globally renowned actor and professional wrestler, celebrated for his charisma, athleticism, and successful crossover from WWE to blockbuster films.

Early Life and Background Dwayne Douglas Johnson was born on May 2, 1972, in Hayward, California. He is the son of Rocky Johnson, a WWE Hall of Fame wrestler, and Mataniufeagaimaleata “Ata” Fitisemanu (née Maivia). Johnson has Samoan ancestry on his mother’s side and Black Nova Scotian heritage with a small amount of Irish ancestry from his father. Growing up, he briefly lived in New Zealand before returning to the United States, where he excelled in football and wrestling during his high school years

Football and College Johnson received a full scholarship to play football at the University of Miami, where he was part of the 1991 national championship team, mainly as a backup defensive tackle. After college, he briefly joined the Calgary Stampeders in the Canadian Football League, but was released before playing a full season
